Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Just a Little Leisure account and the data stored with it. App developer: [YOUR NAME / STUDIO].

Option 1 - inside the game (fastest)

  1. Open the game and tap the ⚙️ Settings button.
  2. Tap Delete account & cloud data.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

Option 2 - by email

If you can no longer open the game, email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you registered with, with the subject Delete my account. We will delete your account and cloud save within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email address and password) from Firebase Authentication.
  • Your cloud save (progress backup, device identifier and timestamps) from Cloud Firestore.

What is kept

  • Progress stored only on your own device is not on our servers; it stays on your phone until you use Settings → Wipe local data or uninstall the game.
  • Routine server logs and backups held by Google may persist for a short period before being automatically removed.
