The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

1. Playing without an account

You can play without creating an account. Your progress is then stored only on your device. We do not receive it.

2. If you create an account (optional)

Creating an account lets you back up your progress and continue on another phone. We then collect and store:

  • Email address and password - used to sign you in. Handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your password in readable form.
  • Game save data - your progress (level, pets, items, settings in the game), stored in Google Firestore.
  • An anonymous device identifier - a random code generated by the game, used only to allow the account to be signed in on one device at a time.
  • Timestamps - when the save was last uploaded.

We use this data only to provide the sign-in and cloud-save feature. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Service providers

Account and cloud-save data is processed on our behalf by Google (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), under Google's Firebase privacy terms. Data may be processed on servers outside your country.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data until you delete your account. You can delete your account and cloud save at any time:

  • In the game: Settings → Delete account & cloud data, or
  • On the web: see how to delete your account.

Deleting the account removes your login and your cloud save. Progress stored on your own device stays there until you wipe it (Settings → Wipe local data) or uninstall the game.
